# Product

## Platform
Web: React 19, Vite, Tailwind CSS, deployed on Vercel.

## Purpose
Viraj Induruwa's personal portfolio. Help clients and recruiters understand his backend engineering, enterprise automation, applied machine learning and full-stack work, explore projects, download his resume and contact him.

## Confirmed scope
The user requests a substantial, distinctive, animated redesign guided by four ranked reference portfolios. Fonts and colors may change. Avoid juvenile ASCII/particle treatments and generic AI-looking layouts. No em dashes in new content.

## Content and behavior
Retain real supplied project records, career facts, certification assets, social links, resume download and existing contact integration. Do not invent proof or send test messages. Local preview is http://localhost:5173/. Deployment is separate from local implementation.
